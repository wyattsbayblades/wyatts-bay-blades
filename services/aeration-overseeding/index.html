Lawn Aeration & Overseeding

Compacted soil and thin turf can make it difficult for grass to establish. Wyatt's Bay Blades offers aeration and overseeding as part of lawn improvement and restoration projects when conditions and timing are appropriate.

Request an Estimate

Why Aerate?

Aeration creates openings in compacted soil that can improve air, water and nutrient movement around the root zone. Overseeding can add new grass plants to thin areas when the timing and seedbed are right.

Timing matters.
Grass type, weather, soil conditions and existing turf all affect the best approach. We evaluate the property before recommending a restoration plan.

Part of a Complete Lawn Plan

Aeration and overseeding can be combined with lawn restoration, proper mowing and other maintenance practices. The goal is to establish healthier, denser turf rather than simply perform a single treatment.
