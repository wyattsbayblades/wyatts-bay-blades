Lawn Restoration

Thin, damaged or struggling lawns often need more than another mowing. Wyatt's Bay Blades provides lawn restoration work designed around the condition of the existing turf and soil.

A Restoration Approach

1. Assess

We look at turf condition, soil, compaction, sunlight and the property's existing conditions.

2. Prepare

Depending on the project, preparation can include correcting surface conditions, improving the seedbed and addressing existing vegetation.

3. Seed & Establish

Where appropriate, we can use overseeding or other restoration methods suited to the lawn and season.

4. Maintain

Newly restored turf needs proper mowing and care while it establishes. We can discuss an ongoing maintenance plan.
