Lawn Restoration

A Better Lawn Starts Below the Grass.

When a lawn is thin, compacted, patchy or struggling, simply throwing down seed isn't always enough.


We look at the actual conditions of the property and work through the process of preparing the soil, establishing seed and giving the new lawn the best possible chance to succeed.

01

Evaluate

Look at the lawn, soil, sunlight, drainage and existing conditions.

02

Prepare

Address compaction, thatch and other conditions preventing healthy growth.

03

Seed

Select appropriate seed and establish good seed-to-soil contact.

04

Establish

Watering and follow-up care are critical to getting new grass established.
