Who We Are

Experience behind the work.

Wyatt's Bay Blades officially launched in 2026, but the experience behind the business goes back much further.

Patrick began working at Homestead Gardens in 2011, developing hands-on knowledge of plants, soil, mulch, lawn care, landscape materials and professional equipment.

That experience continued into field work with Hillis-Carnes Engineering Associates, where Patrick expanded his understanding of soil composition, compaction, drainage and the importance of proper preparation and construction techniques.

Today, Wyatt's Bay Blades brings that practical experience together with a technical, detail-oriented approach to improving outdoor spaces.

The goal isn't just to make a property look better today. It's to understand what the property needs and do the work the right way.

What We Do

Lawn care & landscaping services

Lawn Restoration

Lawn Mowing & Maintenance
