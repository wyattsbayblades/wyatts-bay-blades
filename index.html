Our Story

Built With Purpose.
Built For Family.

Wyatt's Bay Blades was built from a simple idea: take the pride, attention to detail and work ethic we bring to our own property and bring that same standard to yours.

As a family-owned business on Maryland's Eastern Shore, we understand that your property is more than just grass and landscaping. It is where your family lives, where memories are made and where you take pride in your home.

We started Wyatt's Bay Blades to build something of our own — a company that could provide dependable work for our neighbors while creating something meaningful for our family. Every project is treated like it matters, because it does.

From routine mowing and maintenance to larger landscape cleanups, mulch and soil work, and complete lawn restoration, our goal is simple: leave your property looking better than we found it.

Why Wyatt's Bay Blades?

We're not trying to be the biggest landscaping company. We're focused on being the company our customers can count on — showing up, doing quality work and treating every property with respect.

Our name comes from our family and our home on the Eastern Shore. The Chesapeake Bay is part of what makes this area special, and we wanted our company to reflect that connection.

Lawn Restoration

Give Your Lawn
A Fresh Start.

When a lawn is thin, compacted, damaged or struggling, mowing more often isn't the answer. We evaluate the property and build a restoration plan around what the lawn actually needs.

01

Assess

Evaluate soil, sunlight, drainage, compaction and existing turf conditions.

02

Prepare

Prepare the soil through appropriate cleanup, dethatching, aeration and amendments.

03

Seed

Select appropriate seed and establish new turf with proper application and starter fertilizer.

04

Establish

Provide watering guidance and follow-up recommendations to help the new lawn establish.
